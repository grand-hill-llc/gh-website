How we work

From walkthrough to handoff.

Work is carried out by prequalified specialty crews that carry their own insurance, under Grand Hill's direction and inspection. We answer for the result.

  1. Scope

    We walk the site, confirm surfaces, substrates and finish specifications, and note access constraints.

  2. Estimate

    A clear written estimate with scope, exclusions and schedule. No surprises in the numbers.

  3. Execute

    A prequalified crew works to a schedule built around the building and its occupants.

  4. Inspect

    We inspect against the specification, correct anything that falls short and hand off.
