Our story

Four disciplines. One team.

Grand Hill brings architecture, engineering, finance and operations into one team, so every project is planned, priced and delivered with the same care.

We came together in Orlando around one commitment: executing high-quality projects. Each of us owns a part of the work, and together we answer for the result.

  • Architecture

    Experience leading large-scale projects, from design intent to completion.

  • Engineering

    An eye for how a building is built and how finishes perform on it.

  • Finance

    Clear estimates and honest numbers, with no surprises.

  • Operations

    Systems and communication that keep you informed from walkthrough to handoff.

How we work

From walkthrough to handoff.

Every project is planned, directed and inspected by Grand Hill, from the first walkthrough to the final handoff. We answer for the result.

You have one point of contact throughout. Scope, finishes and schedule are confirmed in writing before work begins.

Request a quote

  1. Scope

    We walk the site, confirm surfaces, substrates and finish specifications, and note access constraints.

  2. Estimate

    A clear written estimate with scope, exclusions and schedule. No surprises in the numbers.

  3. Execute

    The work follows a schedule built around the building and its occupants.

  4. Inspect

    We inspect against the specification, correct anything that falls short and hand off.
