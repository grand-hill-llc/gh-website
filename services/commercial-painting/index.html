Our process

How a commercial project runs.

  1. Walk and scope

    We visit the site, confirm access and hours, and write the scope down.

  2. Sequence

    Work is planned around operations, tenants and deliveries.

  3. Prepare and coat

    Surfaces are cleaned, repaired and primed, then coated to specification.

  4. Inspect and hand off

    Each area is checked against the scope before it is handed back.
