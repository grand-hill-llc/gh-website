How we work

The same process on every project.

  1. Scope

    We walk the site, confirm surfaces, substrates and finish specifications, and note access constraints.

  2. Estimate

    A clear written estimate with scope, exclusions and schedule. No surprises in the numbers.

  3. Execute

    The work follows a schedule built around the building and its occupants.

  4. Inspect

    We inspect against the specification, correct anything that falls short and hand off.
