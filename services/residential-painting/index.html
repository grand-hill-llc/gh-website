Our process

How a home project runs.

  1. Walk and scope

    We walk the property with you and write down the scope and colors.

  2. Protect and prepare

    Floors and furniture are protected, and surfaces are cleaned, repaired and primed.

  3. Paint

    Coats are applied to specification, one area at a time.

  4. Final walkthrough

    We check the finish against the scope before handoff.
