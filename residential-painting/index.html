What we paint

Inside and out.

  • Interior painting

    Walls, ceilings and trim, finished to one consistent standard.

  • Exterior and stucco

    Surfaces prepared and coated to stand up to Florida sun, humidity and rain.

  • Cleaning and preparation

    Pressure washing, patching and priming before any coat goes on.

  • Repairs and finishing details

    Caulking, sealing and touch-ups checked before handoff.

Our process

How a home project runs.

  1. Walk and scope

    We walk the property with you and write down the scope and colors.

  2. Protect and prepare

    Floors and furniture are protected, and surfaces are cleaned, repaired and primed.

  3. Paint

    Coats are applied to specification, one area at a time.

  4. Final walkthrough

    We check the finish against the scope before handoff.
