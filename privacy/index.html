Privacy policy.

How Grand Hill, LLC handles the information you share with us. Effective November 18, 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by Grand Hill, LLC ("Grand Hill", "we", "us"), a Florida company. You can reach us at info@grandhillcorp.com.

What we collect

Information you send us. When you request a quote, we receive your name, email address, project city, the service you are asking about, your project details, and your phone number if you choose to give it. We use it to respond to your request, prepare an estimate and manage the project if you hire us.

Information collected automatically. We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. It records things like the pages you visit, approximate location (city level), device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. It uses cookies or similar technologies to do this. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Technical logs. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address and request time, for security and reliability.

How we use it

We use the information to answer your inquiry, provide estimates and services, operate and improve the website, keep it secure, and meet legal and accounting obligations. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site and handle your request, such as website hosting and form processing (Netlify), analytics (Google), and email. We may also share what is needed to carry out your project, and where required by law. These providers may only use the information for the services they perform for us.

Cookies and analytics choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The site works the same way if you do.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and project records for as long as needed to respond to you, complete any work and meet business and legal requirements, and then delete or anonymize them. Analytics data is kept for the period set in our Google Analytics account.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, by emailing info@grandhillcorp.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and we limit access to the information you send us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This site is intended for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Other websites

Our pages link to other sites, such as Instagram and Facebook. Their privacy practices are their own, and this policy does not cover them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
